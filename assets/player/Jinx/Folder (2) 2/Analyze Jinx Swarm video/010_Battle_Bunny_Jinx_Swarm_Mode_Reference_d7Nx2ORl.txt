## Key Design Elements Observed

### Character Silhouette (Top-Down)
- Slim, agile body type
- Long twin-tails pigtails visible from above
- Compact, readable silhouette at isometric view
- Shoulder-mounted weapons extend the character width

### Weapons
- **Gatling gun (Pow-Pow / "Meow Meow" in Swarm):** Held in front/side, multi-barrel spinning gun with chunky futuristic design
- **Rocket launcher (Fishbones):** Large shark-head missile launcher on one shoulder
- Weapons are oversized relative to body — key design language

### Battle Bunny Costume Details
- **Bunny ears:** White, upright rabbit ears on a pink headband
- **Armor layers:** Pink + white tech plates over a bodysuit
- **Teal/cyan accent color** on visor and weapon details
- **Shoulder pads:** Large, angular tech-wear shoulder armor
- **Boots:** Platform/tech boots with armor plating

### Movement Style (Swarm Mode)
- Top-down isometric perspective, moves in 8 directions
- Auto-fires weapons continuously while alive
- When idle: stands with gun lowered, pigtails visible from above
- When moving: leans forward dynamically

## Context for Game Character Design
The user is designing a **game character inspired by Battle Bunny Jinx** in Swarm mode. Key elements to reference:
1. Top-down readable silhouette — wide shoulder pads + bunny ears create iconic shape
2. Tech-wear armor layers (not just a bunny costume — it's futuristic battle gear with bunny motifs)
3. Oversized weapons that extend beyond the character body
4. Color palette: Pink (#FF69B4), White (#F5F5F5), Teal (#00CED1) with dark bodysuit base
5. Twin pigtails — visible and bouncy from top-down view
6. Chunky, game-readable details that read clearly at small screen sizes
